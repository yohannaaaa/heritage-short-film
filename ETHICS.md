# ETHICS — The Heritage Short

*At One Table, In Different Worlds (በአንድ ጠረጴዛ፣ በተለያዩ ዓለማት)*

## 1. Consent record

**No real person's face, likeness or voice appears in this film.**

- All characters (mother, uncle, two sisters, adult narrator) are **fictional characters**. They are inspired by my personal family memories, but they are not intended as visual replicas of any real family member. See `character_policy` in `storyboard.json`.
- All images are generated from text prompts only. **No photographs of real people were uploaded, used as reference images, or used for image-to-image generation.**
- No real person's voice was recorded, cloned or imitated. **No voice cloning was used.** Every voice is a **preset Gemini TTS voice** (Sulafat, Kore, Charon, Aoede), chosen from the model's built-in list.
- The words spoken by the characters are lines I wrote myself in Amharic, based on my own memory of family jokes and conversations.

**Family story consent.** The story is based on real everyday memories of my family.
- I told [mother / uncle / other family members] that I am making this film and what it contains: yes
- Date and how they agreed (verbal, message, in person): verbal
- Anything they asked me to change or leave out: no

**Listeners in the audience tests.** The three people who heard the radio cut and the near-final cut gave their feedback voluntarily. Their names are not published. Only their role (e.g. "cousin, 24") appears in DESIGN.md.

## 4. Style and identity

- Prompts describe qualities (warm, golden light, cinematic, realistic), never the name of a living artist, director or photographer.
- No celebrity, brand or copyrighted character appears in any prompt.
- Text appears only in code-made subtitles and titles, not inside generated images.

## 5. Cultural care

- The film shows ordinary Ethiopian family life: the shared **tiri**, shiro and injera, and the Wednesday and Friday fasting custom. These details come from my own family experience.
- The film does not blame technology, work or modern life. It asks a reflective question.
- Native Amharic speakers listened to the narration to check pronunciation and naturalness: [name or role, what they changed].

## 6. Known limitations

- Gemini TTS Amharic support is labelled Preview in Google's documentation. Some words may be mispronounced; see FAILURES.md and EXPERIMENTS.md for what I found.
- Generated faces and hands may contain small errors. The characters are fictional and may look different from shot to shot; see continuity notes in `storyboard.json`.
